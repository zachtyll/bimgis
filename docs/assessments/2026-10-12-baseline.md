# Assessment 2026-10-12: baseline

This assessment applies the design framework from the thesis ([docs/inspo/thesis.typ](../inspo/thesis.typ), § Design framework) to bimgis as it stands today: a web viewer with an OSM basemap, LoD1 buildings for central Luleå, IFC preview, click-to-inspect, and no backend for participation. It uses the same question form as the thesis's Helsinki assessment (Appendix A). Requirement IDs refer to [design/00](../design/00-participation-design-framework.md).

## Summary

Today bimgis is a **Viewer** with a little **Finder**. It covers none of the participation dimensions. Its strengths are open foundations (open-source stack, open formats) and a web-first base that can reach phones. Every *Must* in SOC, KNO and GOV is unmet. That's expected at this stage, and it is the backlog.
